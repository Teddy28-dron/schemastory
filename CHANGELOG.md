## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-19

### Added

- Local JSON, JSONL, and NDJSON sample comparison.
- Bounded profiling of observed paths, types, record coverage, and null ratio.
- Deterministic breaking, warning, and info classifications.
- Terminal, versioned JSON, and standalone interactive HTML reports.
- Explicit CI policies through `--fail-on breaking|warning|never`.
- Privacy regression tests ensuring reports exclude raw input values.
- Cross-platform CLI with actionable errors and stable exit codes.
- Synthetic agent-trace demo, documentation, CI, and community templates.
